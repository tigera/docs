  A troubleshooting page is a how-to guide that starts from a symptom rather than a goal.
  The reader has a problem, often under pressure.
  They want to recognize their symptom, find the cause, and fix it, as fast as possible.

  A troubleshooting page covers one or more symptoms for one feature or area, such as egress gateways or BGP.
  Each symptom has one or more causes, and each cause has a fix.

  If the problems happen only while the reader follows one procedure, don't write a troubleshooting page.
  Add a Troubleshooting section to that how-to guide instead.

  Include a symptom only if it could affect many readers.
  If it happens only under a narrow set of conditions, such as one version on one platform with a particular combination of dependencies, leave it out of the docs.
  Consider a support article instead.

  A troubleshooting page doesn't teach a feature or explain how it works.
  Link to explanation pages and how-to guides instead.

  Good examples in our docs:
  - calico-enterprise/networking/l2-bridge/troubleshoot.mdx
  - calico-enterprise/networking/egress/troubleshoot.mdx
  - calico-cloud/threat/gateway-waf/troubleshooting.mdx

  Read content-types.md for how troubleshooting fits with the other content types.
*/}
