  An installation guide is a how-to guide for installing a product on one platform or with one install method.
  It's often the first task a reader does with the product, so it has to work the first time.
  The reader is installing into their own environment, and they need to know what to prepare, what to run, and how to tell that it worked.

  An installation guide doesn't explain the product or compare platforms.
  Link to explanation pages and to the system requirements instead.

  Good examples in our docs:
  - calico-enterprise/getting-started/install-on-clusters/nkp.mdx
  - calico-enterprise/installation/install-calico-enterprise-mke-4k.mdx
  - calico-cloud/get-started/install-cluster.mdx

*/}
